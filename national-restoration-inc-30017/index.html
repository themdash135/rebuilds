Category guide — general information

Hail and storm damage: how roof claims generally work.

Read this as general background on the trade, not as a description of National Restoration's own process. Metro Atlanta sits in hail country and this is the sequence homeowners are typically walked through after a storm. What this company does on your specific job is a question for the phone call — the script is below.

An inspection, before the insurer

Hail bruising and wind-lifted shingles are usually invisible from the ground, so the first step is generally someone getting on the roof and photographing what is there.

Damage documented in writing

Claims typically turn on evidence: dated photographs, a written scope of the damage, and the storm date. Contractors normally supply this so the homeowner isn't describing a roof from memory.

The adjuster's visit

Insurers usually send their own adjuster. Homeowners commonly ask their contractor to be on site for that visit so both parties are measuring the same roof.

Nothing on this page states what any insurer will approve, what a deductible will be, or what a roof will cost. Prices, insurance handling and lead times are not published anywhere by this company, so they are not guessed at here. Ask them directly — the exact questions are below.

Before you hire anyone

What to ask when you call (404) 271-3254.

Prices, warranties, licensing paperwork and insurance handling are not published, so ask on the phone. These are the questions worth asking any roofer who knocks on your door in Gwinnett County, this one included.

  • 01Do you do roof repairs, or only full replacements?
  • 02Are you licensed and insured — can you email me the certificate of insurance?
  • 03Is the inspection free, and is there any obligation after it?
  • 04Do you handle insurance claims, and will you be here when the adjuster comes?
  • 05What warranty covers your workmanship, and what does the shingle manufacturer cover?
  • 06Which shingle brand are you quoting, and what grade of shingle is it?
  • 07How soon could you start, and how many days is the job on site?
  • 08Does the quote include tear-off, disposal, and a magnet sweep of the yard?
  • 09Who is on site running the job, and how do I reach that person?
  • 10Can you also quote the gutters, siding or paintwork while you are up there?
