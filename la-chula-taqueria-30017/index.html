Before you drive over

What to ask when you call

Prices, ordering ahead and large orders aren't published online. Sixty seconds on the phone settles all of it — here's the script.

  1. What do the street tacos and the birria run today, per taco and per plate?
  2. Can I place a pickup order over the phone, and how long until it's ready?
  3. Is the kitchen still taking orders at the hour I'd be arriving?
  4. What works best for kids, and for anyone avoiding spice?
  5. For a group order, how much notice do you want?
