Before you call

Six questions worth asking on the phone

Lead times, warranty terms and financing rates are quoted by the company, not published anywhere. Ask directly — and write down the answers so you can compare quotes.

  1. Is the $59 first-system / $49 additional-system inspection price still current?
  2. What does it cost to diagnose a system that is already down, and does that fee come off the repair?
  3. How soon can someone get out to my address, and what is the arrival window?
  4. Do you stock parts for my equipment — Goodman, Daikin, Trane or otherwise?
  5. Is the estimate free and in writing, and what does the financing actually cost per month?
  6. What warranty covers the part, and what covers the labor?
