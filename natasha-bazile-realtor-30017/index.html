Before you commit to any agent

Five questions worth
asking on the first call.

Commission structure, marketing budget and timelines are things no agent's website can answer honestly for your specific home — they depend on the property and the market. So rather than post a number that would be a guess, here is the script. Ask Natasha these, and ask the next agent the same ones.

  • 01

    “Do you work in my part of the county, and how recently have you closed near me?”

  • 02

    “What is your commission, what does it cover, and what is negotiable?”

  • 03

    “What would you list my house at, and what is that number based on?”

  • 04

    “Who pays for photography, staging and marketing — and what exactly gets produced?”

  • 05

    “When I call or text, am I reaching you or an assistant, and how long is a typical reply?”
