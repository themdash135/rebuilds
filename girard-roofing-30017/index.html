Roof leak repair

Leaks rarely start where the ceiling stain is. Chimney chases, rusted cap flashing, vent boots and valleys are the usual entry points, and finding the real one is the job. Roof leaks are the service Girard has led with on its own site for years.

General guidance · not this company's policies

What to ask any roofer in Gwinnett County before you hire.

Prices, insurance details and how soon a crew can start are not published anywhere by this business, so this page does not guess at them. These are the questions worth putting to any roofing contractor on the first call — the answers should come from them, not from a website.

Roofing contractors in general vary widely on all five of these. Ask, and write the answers down.
Ask thisWhy it matters
“Is this a repair, and what would a replacement cost instead?” Get both numbers in the same conversation. A quote that only exists in one direction tells you nothing about whether the other was ever considered.
“Can you send proof of liability insurance and workers' comp?” If someone is hurt on your roof, whose policy responds is a question you want answered before the ladder goes up, not after.
“What's the warranty on the workmanship, and separately on the materials?” These are two different warranties from two different parties. A single vague “guaranteed” covers neither.
“What do you need from me for an insurance claim, and will you meet the adjuster?” After hail or wind, the paperwork sequence matters as much as the repair. Establish who documents what before work starts.
“What's the deposit, and what does the written estimate include?” A written scope — materials, tear-off, decking, disposal, and what happens if rot is found underneath — is the difference between a price and a surprise.
