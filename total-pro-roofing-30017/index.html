THE MATH, AS THEY PUBLISH IT

ACV + DEDUCTIBLE + DEPRECIATION = RCV

Actual cash value plus your deductible plus recoverable depreciation equals replacement cost value. It is the single equation that governs what a storm claim pays out, and it is the headline on Total Pro Roofing’s homeowner-insurance page.
