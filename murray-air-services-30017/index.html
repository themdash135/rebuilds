Before and during the call

General guidance for booking any HVAC contractor.

Nothing below is a Murray Air Services policy — pricing, warranty terms and scheduling windows are set by the company and aren't published anywhere we can quote. This is the standard shape of an HVAC service call, and the questions worth asking before anyone drives out.

Have this ready when you dial

  1. 1
    Equipment type and brandRead the label on the outdoor unit or the furnace door — brand, model, serial. It's usually a sticker.
  2. 2
    Roughly how old it isAge is the difference between a repair conversation and a replacement conversation.
  3. 3
    The exact symptomNot "it's broken" — runs but blows warm, won't start at all, starts then stops after two minutes, makes a noise it didn't make last week.
  4. 4
    What you already checkedThermostat setting and batteries, the breaker, the filter, the outdoor unit's disconnect. Saying so saves everyone a step.
  5. 5
    Access detailsAttic, crawlspace, closet, roof — and a gate code or dog, if there is one.

Ask these while you're on the phone

Rates, warranties and scheduling aren't published — so ask, and write the answers down.

  • "What's the diagnostic or trip charge, and is it applied to the repair if I go ahead?"
  • "Which brands do you install, and what do you keep on the truck for a system like mine?"
  • "What warranty comes with the labor, and what comes with the part?"
  • "What's the earliest window you can get to me, and will I get a call before arrival?"
  • "Can you confirm the license number on file — CN211147 — and that you're insured for the work?"
  • "If it needs replacing instead of repairing, will you quote both so I can compare?"
